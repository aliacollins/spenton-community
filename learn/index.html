What is SpentOn?

SpentOn is a comprehensive, open-source envelope budgeting app. Plan with money you have, track bills and subscriptions, and keep shared expenses connected to your personal budget. Repayments stay separate from income.

You add balances and transactions manually or review a CSV import. SpentOn does not connect to banks or send money. Your personal accounts and budgets require sign-in.

How much does it cost?

The upcoming self-hosted release is free software. You provide the server and any external services. Cloud is the optional paid service for hosting, updates, backups and support. Try Cloud free for 40 days, with no card required or automatic charge. Planned pricing is US$7.99/month or US$79/year. Paid checkout is disabled during beta; after the trial, viewing and downloading remain available.
