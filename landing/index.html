Questions about SpentOn

Before you choose.

What’s available, what’s free and how the two hosting options work.

What is SpentOn?

SpentOn is a comprehensive envelope budgeting app for planning spending, tracking subscriptions, tracking cash set aside for card payments and splitting bills. Cloud is in beta; the free, open-source self-hosted beta under AGPL-3.0-only is available on GitHub.

Is SpentOn free and open source?

Self-hosted SpentOn is free software under AGPL-3.0-only. Download the public beta from GitHub and follow the short Docker setup guide. SpentOn Cloud is the paid managed option, with a 40-day beta trial and planned pricing of $7.99/month or $79/year.

Do self-hosting and Cloud have the same features?

Both include the same budgeting features and a limit of three budgets. Self-hosting does not require a Cloud subscription or activation. You operate your own installation. Local accounts need no email service; optional receipt processing uses your own provider. Shared bills and groups work within one server; separate servers do not share bills with each other.

Why pay for SpentOn Cloud?

Cloud is for people who want to use SpentOn without maintaining a server. The managed offer covers hosting, updates, backups and support. Choose self-hosting if you prefer to operate those yourself.

What happens after the Cloud trial?

There is no permanent free hosted tier. After 40 days, editing pauses while viewing and downloading saved budgets remain available. Paid checkout is not open during beta, and the trial does not charge you automatically. Planned monthly subscriptions renew monthly; the $79 annual option is one payment with no automatic renewal.

Can I install it with one command?

Yes. With Docker and Compose installed, run the start command from the source folder. SpentOn generates its settings automatically. Open localhost:8080 and create a local username and password. No email service, domain or separate Node.js installation is needed.

Can I use the official app with my own server?

The iPhone app is coming soon, with support for a compatible HTTPS SpentOn server. This capability is in testing; the app is not yet available in the App Store. You can use SpentOn Cloud in desktop and phone browsers now.

Does SpentOn connect to my bank or scan receipts?

SpentOn does not connect to banks or send payments. Add transactions yourself or review a CSV import. Optional receipt scanning requires a configured processing provider and your consent. Self-hosted operators supply and pay for their own provider; scanning is not included merely by installing the software.

Can I move my data between servers?

Independent personal budgets can be exported and imported. Switching the server in the mobile app does not move data. Shared bills, group memberships and account identities do not move through a personal-budget import. A full server backup is used to restore an installation.

What does AGPL-3.0-only allow?

AGPLv3 permits using, modifying and redistributing the covered software, including compliant paid hosting. Its source-sharing obligations apply where required. SpentOn’s name, Pip artwork and recordings have separate permissions; private Cloud billing and operations are outside the public source package.
