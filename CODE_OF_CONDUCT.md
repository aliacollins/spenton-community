# SpentOn community guidelines

SpentOn welcomes people with different backgrounds, budgets and levels of
technical experience. You do not need to be a developer to contribute.

## How we work together

- Be respectful and specific. Discuss the idea or behavior, without attacking the person.
- Make room for beginners. Explain an unfamiliar step and link to useful information.
- Treat financial circumstances as private. Do not judge someone's income, debt, spending or ability to pay.
- Ask before sharing someone else's work or personal details. Use fictional data in examples.
- Keep discussions relevant, and give maintainers time to respond. A request is not a promise of delivery.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual harassment, deliberate intimidation,
publication of private information and repeated disruptive promotion are not
welcome. Do not use community spaces to solicit money or sell financial schemes.

## Reporting a concern

Email **privacy@spenton.dev** with “SpentOn community concern” in the subject.
Include links and a short account of what happened. Share only the personal
information needed to understand the report. Security vulnerabilities belong
in the private process described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers will assess reports and limit disclosure to what is needed to
address them. Depending on the behavior and its impact, they may request an
edit, remove content, give a warning or temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. Serious threats or disclosure of private data may require
immediate action. Explain relevant context or appeal a decision through the
same email address; no particular outcome or response time is guaranteed.

These guidelines apply to this repository, its issues and pull requests, and
project-managed community spaces. Platform rules also apply, including Reddit's
rules when participating in r/Spenton.
