Where did the rest of the money go?

You started with $500, with $300 set aside for Bills, $120 for Dining and $80 available to plan. Paying the $120 dinner leaves $380 in the bank. Bills still has $300 set aside and Dining has $80 left. The $80 you paid for friends is outstanding, so it is no longer available to plan.

Receiving a repayment brings cash back into the account and reduces what friends owe you. It does not create new income or change your $40 share of dinner. In your real budget, make or check payments through your usual bank or payment service, then record what happened.

This page uses fixed fictional records. It does not read your accounts, contact friends or save a budget.
